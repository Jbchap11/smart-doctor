About CarePoint Hospital

A legacy of compassionate care, clinical excellence, and modern medical technology.

Our Mission & Vision

At CarePoint Hospital, our mission is to ensure every patient receives prompt, accurate, and empathetic medical attention. Smart Doctor was conceived to bridge the gap between patient symptoms and appropriate physician scheduling, ensuring urgent cases are not lost in standard waitlists.

Digital Health Assessment Philosophy

Patients complete a 10-point clinical assessment that identifies symptoms requiring immediate clinical evaluation versus routine preventive checkups.

🤖 AI-Powered Smart Triage by CarePoint

CarePoint Hospital uses an in-house Machine Learning (ML) model — trained on thousands of real clinical triage cases — to intelligently assess each patient's urgency level before an appointment is booked.

Our AI engine, Smart Doctor, analyses 10 clinical parameters including primary health concern, symptom duration, pain intensity, neurological signs, and pre-existing conditions. It then:

  • Classifies the case as Very Urgent, Moderate, or Routine
  • Automatically matches the patient to the right specialist
  • Assigns a prioritised appointment slot — no manual selection needed
  • Gives the patient a choice of tomorrow or later based on real doctor availability

Developed in collaboration with the hospital's clinical informatics and data science team. For emergencies, please call 1800-CAREPOINT or visit the nearest emergency unit.
